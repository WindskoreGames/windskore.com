Return Policy

Windskore – Digital products

1. General

This return policy applies to purchases of digital products, gift cards and services from Windskore (windskore.com).

2. Digital products

Digital products such as games, DLC and software are generally not eligible for return once delivery has started (e.g. download or activation), in accordance with applicable consumer law for digital content.

If a product is defective or does not match the description, please contact us and we will help resolve the issue.

3. Gift cards

Purchased gift cards can be returned within 14 days if they have not been used or partially redeemed. Contact support@windskore.com for assistance.

4. How to request a return

Email support@windskore.com with your order number, the product, and the reason for the return. We aim to respond within a few business days.

5. Refunds

Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method within a reasonable time (usually 5–14 business days depending on the payment provider).
